# Competitive Analysis Plan

Date: 2026-07-01

## Goal

Determine whether VibeReader Knowledge Workbench should keep the Reader-first + Local Knowledge Flywheel positioning, or shift toward ChatPDF, generic RAG knowledge base, or note-taking software.

## Scope

First-round competitor set:

- NotebookLM
- Zotero
- Readwise Reader
- ChatPDF or equivalent PDF chat product
- Obsidian + AI/RAG workflow
- AnythingLLM or equivalent local RAG product

## Out of Scope

- Exhaustive market map.
- Pricing optimization.
- Enterprise buyer research.
- Full user interviews.
- Feature copying without product reasoning.

## Evidence Requirements

Use current sources wherever possible:

- Official site.
- Official docs.
- Pricing page.
- Changelog or product release notes.
- Real product trial or screenshots where available.

Because competitor information changes, formal analysis must use browser/web verification before conclusions are treated as product facts.

## Analysis Dimensions

For each product:

- Target user.
- Core job.
- First-run experience.
- Reading experience.
- AI/RAG capability.
- Citation and evidence quality.
- Knowledge retention.
- Privacy and local-first posture.
- Model flexibility.
- Pricing / packaging.
- What we should learn.
- What we should avoid.
- Implication for VibeReader.

## Planning Questions

1. Is Reader-first still the correct first-screen product posture?
2. Which competitor has the strongest onboarding lesson?
3. Which competitor has the strongest citation/evidence lesson?
4. Which competitor has the strongest knowledge-retention loop?
5. What should become P0 for VibeReader?
6. What should explicitly remain out of scope?

## User Confirmation Needed

Confirm after research, before roadmap changes:

- Whether to keep Reader-first positioning.
- Whether the first P0 should be RAG adapter, citation jump, or UX/onboarding.
- Which competitor patterns to learn from.
- Which patterns to reject.
