# Idea

Use yishuship to manage the lifecycle of the unified VibeReader + UniRAG product.

Product direction:

- VibeReader is the reading entrance.
- UniRAG is the long-term local knowledge memory.
- A user reads documents, highlights, asks questions, saves notes and cards.
- Valuable user-confirmed artifacts flow into RAG.
- The system becomes a local knowledge flywheel: every reading session improves future retrieval, questioning, citation, and learning.
